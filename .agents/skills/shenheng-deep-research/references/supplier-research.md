# Supplier Research Reference

## Supplier evidence levels

### Confirmed
A current or recent source directly identifies the supplier/exporter and the target company.

### Strongly supported
Customs/trade data identifies repeated transactions between the parties, with matching company identity and product.

### Possible
Origin/product patterns, distributor statements, or indirect references suggest a relationship but do not establish it conclusively.

## Research sequence
1. Search target company + product + supplier/exporter names.
2. Check customs/trade data for exporter and origin patterns.
3. Check the supplier's official site for distributor/customer references.
4. Check the buyer's site/social channels for supplier or brand references.
5. Reconcile addresses, domains, legal names, and subsidiaries.
6. Label the result as confirmed, strongly supported, possible, or unverified.

## Avoid
- Treating a supplier directory as proof of an active supplier relationship.
- Assuming the largest exporter is the supplier.
- Treating an old shipment as a current relationship without a date check.
