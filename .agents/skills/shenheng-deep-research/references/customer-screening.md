# Customer Screening Reference

## Default priority

**High priority**
- Relevant steel importer/distributor/stockist
- Repeated or recent import activity
- Relevant Shen Heng product match
- China-import evidence or credible alternative sourcing pattern
- Procurement contact identifiable
- No major trade barrier that destroys competitiveness

**Medium priority**
- Strong steel demand but limited import evidence
- Product fit is clear but sourcing/contact evidence is incomplete
- Potential processor or project-oriented buyer with recurring demand

**Low priority**
- Generic construction-material retailer with no steel-import evidence
- Steel manufacturer when the goal is to sell trading/distribution products
- No verifiable steel demand
- Duplicate or uncertain company identity
- Major tariff/AD barrier with no clear commercial route

## Suggested scoring

Score each category 0–5:
- Product fit
- Import evidence
- China-origin evidence
- Recency/frequency
- Company type fit
- Contactability
- Logistics fit
- Trade-barrier attractiveness

Use the score as a prioritization aid, not as a substitute for evidence.

## Development angle
After qualification, identify one verified product/demand signal and use it to tailor the first message. Keep outreach short. Position Shen Heng as a reliable alternative/backup supplier when appropriate.
