# Company Research Reference

## Goal
Determine whether a company is a real, commercially relevant steel buyer/importer and whether it is worth developing.

## Minimum evidence set
- Legal/business identity
- Country and operating address
- Official domain or other authoritative presence
- Business model
- Steel products handled or consumed
- Import evidence when available
- Procurement/contact route

## Strong signals
- Customs records showing relevant steel products
- Repeated imports rather than a single old shipment
- China as an origin country
- Product descriptions matching Shen Heng products
- Company website explicitly listing steel trading/stocking/distribution
- Procurement or purchasing contact publicly identified

## Weak signals
- Generic “construction materials” wording
- A directory listing with no current company evidence
- Social-media posts without company ownership verification
- A website that only mentions steel as one of many unrelated materials

## Supplier verification
Use “documented supplier” only when a source directly supports the relationship. Use “possible supplier” when based on indirect evidence such as product/origin patterns.
