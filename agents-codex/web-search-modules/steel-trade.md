# Steel Trade Research Module

Use this module for steel market, customs, importer, supplier, HS-code, tariff, anti-dumping, safeguard and trade-flow research.

## Source priority
1. Customs administrations and official tariff/trade-remedy notices
2. UN Comtrade / WITS and other official statistical systems
3. Official company websites and verified company profiles
4. Reputable trade databases with shipment-level or customs-derived records
5. Industry associations and established trade publications
6. Search snippets, directories and social media only as leads, not sole proof for material claims

## Query strategy
Generate variations using:
- Product name + HS code
- Product name + country + import
- Product name + country + China
- Company name + import/export
- Company name + supplier/origin
- Company name + product specification
- Country + anti-dumping / safeguard / tariff + product

## Steel product mapping
Consider, when relevant:
- HRC / hot rolled coil
- CRC / cold rolled coil
- GI / GL / galvanized steel
- PPGI / PPGL / pre-painted steel
- GI strip / PPGI strip
- Equal angle
- U channel / UPN / UPE
- IPE / IPEAA
- H beam
- Flat bar
- Wire rod

Do not assume a broad HS72 total represents demand for a specific Shen Heng product. Product-level conclusions require product-level evidence.

## Customs-data controls
- State HS code, reporting country, year and unit.
- Do not combine quantity and value from different years.
- Clearly label mirror/export data.
- Calculate China share only when numerator and denominator are comparable.
- If two databases disagree, show the discrepancy rather than averaging silently.

## Customer qualification
A strong target normally has at least two independent signals: relevant imported product evidence and a website/profile showing distribution, stockholding, trading or processing activity. Contact data should be separately verified.
